Masque Manager — Report

Prototype date: 2026-04-10

Validation

Checked:

  • Self-contained playable index.html with instant instructions
  • Complete loop: sort guests → score round → escalate through 3 rounds
  • Scoring, badges, explicit win and game-over states, plus immediate feedback for each drop
  • Distinct mechanic: social drag-sort triage instead of grid or reaction templates

Concept

A nightclub fantasy about reading people quickly. The player is not matching colors—they are decoding social energy and placing guests into the right vibe ecosystem under pressure.

Why it can hook

It lands instantly because dragging humans into themed rooms is intuitive. The deeper layer—VIP exceptions, mood reads, and “one bad table placement wrecked the round”—creates sticky retries.

Strategy

Frame it as stylish chaos with a readability-first UX. Strong ad material comes from a queue of eccentric guests and the satisfying snap of correct placement.

SEO / ASO / SEM

drag and drop gameparty management gamecasual sorting puzzlenightclub strategy gamevibe matching game

Ad angle: “Can you read the room faster than everyone else?”

Core loop

  1. Read the round twist and lounge identities.
  2. Inspect guest mask, mood, and VIP status.
  3. Drag each guest into a lounge.
  4. Score the round and absorb the panic/hype swing.
  5. Finish 3 rounds before the house implodes.

Rewards

Correct-read dopamine, perfect-round badges, stylish fantasy, and highly shareable character designs.

KPIs

  • Perfect-round rate
  • Average panic at end of round 1
  • Guest misclassification rate by archetype
  • 3-round completion rate
  • Badge unlock frequency

Experiments

  • Daily celebrity archetypes
  • Rule modifiers that temporarily invert one lounge
  • Meta progression with décor themes and bouncer perks
  • Combo chains for consecutive correct VIP placements

Risks

  • Needs more guest archetype variety for longer retention
  • Should avoid ambiguity that feels arbitrary rather than witty
  • Touch UX on small screens must keep drag targets large and forgiving
