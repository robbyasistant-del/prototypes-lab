Chrono Courier — Report

Prototype date: 2026-04-10

Validation

Checked:

  • Self-contained playable index.html with first-screen instructions
  • Complete loop: plan route → auto-run → exact drop → next contract
  • Score, badges, explicit win, explicit game over, and per-action feedback present
  • Distinct mechanic: one-stroke route planning with execution risk, not a clone of swap/match templates

Concept

A delivery puzzle where the fun comes from drawing a path and then watching the courier commit to it. The player gets a very satisfying “I know exactly how to do this better next run” feeling after every failure.

Why it can hook

Low friction: a player understands the loop in seconds. High mastery ceiling: exact parcel routing, anomaly avoidance, and energy economy create a repeatable one-more-try urge.

Strategy

Push the fantasy of being a hyper-efficient station dispatcher. Ads should show the route line drawing, then the bot running it flawlessly for a huge exact-delivery bonus.

SEO / ASO / SEM

route puzzle gamedelivery strategy gameone stroke puzzlesci fi casual gamedaily planning challenge

Creative angle: “Plan once. Pray once. Deliver perfectly.”

Core loop

  1. Read the requested package mix.
  2. Draw an adjacent route from the courier start tile.
  3. Run the route and collect items automatically.
  4. Cash exact-delivery bonuses and badges.
  5. Survive three contracts before energy or stability collapse.

Rewards

Immediate score spikes, exact-drop mastery, clean-route badges, and visible improvement from smarter pathing.

KPIs

  • Run restart rate after first failure
  • Average route length
  • Exact-delivery completion rate
  • Average deliveries per session
  • Share of players who earn at least one badge

Experiments

  • Daily contract seeds
  • Courier classes with different warp traits
  • Powerups that let players redraw mid-run
  • Streak chest after 5 clean deliveries across sessions

Risks

  • Warp logic needs very clear signposting on mobile
  • Difficulty tuning matters: too many dead boards will feel unfair
  • Needs broader mission variety for long-term content
