Starlink Raiders ? Product Report (2026-03-16)

1) Strategy & Trend Fit

Concept grounded in market_intel top terms (stars, wordle, puzzle, daily, merge, music). Positioning: fast daily loop (2-4 min) with high replay and social brag moments.

2) Core Gameplay Loop

  • Learn in 2 seconds via on-screen instruction banner.
  • Take actions with immediate audiovisual feedback.
  • Earn score + unlock badges during run.
  • Reach explicit WIN target or fail into GAME OVER.
  • Restart instantly for "one more try" momentum.

3) Rewards & Progression

  • Dynamic score with combo multipliers.
  • At least 2 badges tied to mastery behavior.
  • Escalating difficulty every wave/round.
  • Session-end clarity with outcome state.

4) SEO / ASO / SEM hooks

  • SEO title variants: "Starlink Raiders daily challenge", "Starlink Raiders puzzle game online".
  • ASO keywords: daily, puzzle, quick challenge, skill game, replay.
  • SEM test creatives: 6-second before/after score clips; CTA "Beat my streak".

5) KPIs to Track

  • D1 return rate
  • Avg session length and restart rate
  • Win rate band (target 25-40%)
  • Badge earn rate
  • Share intent CTA clicks

6) Experiments

  • A/B difficulty ramp (+10% / baseline)
  • Badge cadence (early reward vs delayed mastery)
  • Instruction overlay style (text-only vs animated)

7) Risks

  • Difficulty spikes can reduce retention if unfair.
  • Single-session novelty may decay without daily modifiers.
  • Needs mobile haptics/audio polish for full production fit.
