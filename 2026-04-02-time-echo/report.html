⏰ TIME ECHO

Strategy Report • Prototype 2026-04-02

🎮 Game Overview

Genre: Puzzle / Time Manipulation

Core Mechanic: Your past moves create "echoes" that repeat your actions. Use them to hold buttons, block lasers, and solve puzzles collaboratively with your past selves.

Unique Hook: The first puzzle game where you literally collaborate with your timeline. Every move matters twice.
