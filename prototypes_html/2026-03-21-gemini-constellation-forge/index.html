Forge brief

Connected stars can be chained once each. Close a loop of 4+ stars, trap glowing fragments, and convert them into cores. Empty loops or open shapes spike instability, so choose your geometry carefully.

1 trapped fragment: +1 core and +30 score.
3+ trapped fragments: big bonus and streak.
Bad loop: +14 instability.

    The fantasy is AI-forge as toy: tactile drawing, readable payoff, and greedy risk.
    How to play in 9 seconds

    Draw a loop. Trap fragments.

    Tap stars in sequence. They must be directly linked. Return to the first star to close a loop, then hit Seal loop. Fragments inside the shape become Gemini cores. Reach 6 cores before instability hits 100.
