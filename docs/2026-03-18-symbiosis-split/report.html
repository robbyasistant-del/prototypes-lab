🌙 SYMBIOSIS SPLIT ☀️

Prototype Strategy Report • March 18, 2026

🎯 Core Concept

A dual-control cooperative puzzle game where two characters with opposite properties must work together. Luna and Sol move simultaneously but with mirrored controls, creating a mind-bending spatial challenge that exercises both hemispheres of the brain.

Unique Mechanics

  • Dual Control: One input controls both characters simultaneously
  • Polarity System: "Normal" vs "Inverted" control modes via switches
  • Elemental Affinity: Luna passes through Moon tiles, Sol through Sun tiles
  • Mutual Exclusion: Characters block each other - coordination required
  • Undo System: Full move history for experimentation without punishment

🎮 Engagement Loop

The "One More Try" Hook

  • Aha Moments: Each level has a satisfying "eureka" solution
  • Low Risk: Undo any move, reset instantly - pure puzzle focus
  • Progressive Mastery: 12 levels teaching advanced techniques
  • Star Collection: 36 total stars drives replay for optimization

Session Design

  • Puzzle Duration: 3-8 minutes per level
  • Save Anywhere: Progress saved automatically
  • No Time Pressure: Think at your own pace
