Signal Switchboard Siege

This is a logic sprint, not a static puzzle. Rotate relays so the red, cyan, and gold channels reach the matching sinks before overload burns the switchboard.

  • Click any tile to rotate its connectors
  • All three color paths must be complete at once
  • Clearing a board reduces overload and advances you
  • Win after 3 boards; game over at 100% overload
