Lanterns Below - Product Report

Strategy

Puzzle-forward design for players who enjoy systemic line interactions and near-miss recoveries. Each click is both help and harm: row/column blasts feed altars and rifts simultaneously.

Gameplay Loop

  • Assess line geometry
  • Trigger a lantern
  • Watch row/column charges resolve immediately
  • Monitor altar goals and rift risk
  • Finish in 12 moves or fail fast

Rewards

  • +250 on altar completion
  • Per-move score gain
  • 3 live badges including Deep Keeper win badge

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • SEO: line puzzle game, ritual puzzle browser game, light and shadow puzzle
  • ASO: “Every beam helps and hurts”
  • SEM: visuals of a single click lighting an entire row and column

KPIs

  • Average move count before failure
  • Altar completion rate
  • Rift meltdown frequency
  • Replay rate after near-miss losses

Experiments / Risks

  • Daily puzzle seeds
  • Risk: future versions should surface exact charge math more clearly for experts
