Signal Switchboard Siege — Product Report

A tactile logic-action hybrid inspired by current signals around daily puzzles, logic challenges, and browser-friendly instant play. The player rotates relay tiles to route three live channels to hungry audiences before overload burns the board.

Strategy & Hook

The hook is immediate legibility plus rising pressure. Unlike static pipe puzzles, every move advances a corruption pulse. That creates an addictive risk/reward loop: one more twist could save all channels or fry the grid.

Trend rationale

  • Fresh signals: daily puzzle games, connections-style logic, browser puzzle habits.
  • Designed to feel shareable through near-miss boards and “I solved it with 2% overload left” moments.
  • Low friction, no tutorial wall, clear first-two-second readability.

Core loop

  1. See source colors and target sinks instantly.
  2. Click tiles to rotate paths.
  3. Connect all three channels in time to cash out points and reduce overload.
  4. Survive three boards to win the run.

Scoring, rewards, progression

  • Base points for each connected sink.
  • Time bonus from remaining overload headroom.
  • Badges: Patcher, Tri-Net, Siege Breaker.
  • Win: clear board 3.
  • Game over: overload reaches 100%.

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • Keywords: logic relay puzzle, browser circuit puzzle, rotate tiles game, daily brain teaser.
  • Ad angle: “Can you save the network before the signal implodes?”
  • Store angle: satisfying smart puzzle with pressure and replay value.

KPIs & Experiments

  • Time to first full network clear.
  • Average rotations per win.
  • Retry rate after sub-10% overload losses.
  • Future tests: seeded daily boards, mutator tiles, community challenge mode.

Risks

  • Need long-term board variety beyond current handcrafted topology pool.
  • Could benefit from hint pulses for weaker players on mobile.
