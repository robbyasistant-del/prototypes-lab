Rushline Rooftops — Strategy Report

Concept

An engagement-first browser arcade inspired by strong browser/arcade/mobile micro-session signals. It turns package catching into a lane-switch delivery loop with instant clarity, rising speed, and match-based routing pressure.

Core loop

  • Read portal targets instantly.
  • Move into a lane to catch a parcel.
  • Route that parcel into a matching rooftop portal.
  • Build combo, speed, and delivery streak.
  • Retry after crash because runs fail fast and feel fair.

Rewards and progression

  • Catch and delivery scoring layers
  • Speed escalation that increases tension
  • Combo and clean-run badges
  • Explicit 18-delivery win target

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • SEO: “arcade browser game”, “delivery game online”, “play no download”.
  • ASO: “one more run” messaging, short-run tension, neon rooftop visual hook.
  • SEM: test “fast reflex game”, “3-lane runner”, and “hypercasual browser” creatives.

KPIs

  • Time to first movement
  • Average deliveries per first session
  • Combo peak per run
  • Retry rate after loss
  • Win rate at 18 deliveries

Experiments

  • Daily mission modifiers
  • Two-button mobile controls
  • Ghost rival courier
  • Portal swap warning telegraph to deepen mastery

Risks

  • Needs touch controls for broader mobile reach.
  • Could benefit from larger score pops and SFX later.
  • Difficulty tuning may need softer early ramp for cold users.
