GRAVITY GRAFFITI

Physics Art Studio | April 18, 2026 | Prototype Batch

🎯 Executive Summary

GRAVITY GRAFFITI is a physics puzzle game where players "paint" gravity wells to guide a ball through obstacles to a target zone. The innovative mechanic combines creative expression with strategic problem-solving.

Core Innovation: Players create persistent physics modifiers (attract/repel wells) that remain active, requiring foresight and planning. The "paint" resource creates interesting optimization puzzles.

🎮 Core Game Loop

  1. Analyze: Study the level layout and target position
  2. Plan: Visualize the ball's trajectory
  3. Paint: Spray attract (🧲) or repel (💥) gravity wells
  4. Observe: Watch physics simulation unfold
  5. Iterate: Clear and repaint if needed
  6. Optimize: Complete with minimal paint for 3 stars
