Chorus Chaos - Product Report

Strategy

Party-night inspired solo game that avoids quiz/voting clones. The player reads three audience psychologies and crafts a 3-card act, delivering Jackbox-like energy through systems rather than prompts.

Gameplay Loop

  • Read crowd mood bars
  • Draft exactly 3 cards
  • Resolve act and watch hype swings
  • Earn stars for sweet-spot factions
  • Stabilize or exploit crowd psychology for later acts

Rewards

  • Stars per act
  • Live score scaling with clean reads
  • 3 mastery badges
  • Persistent best score

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • SEO: party strategy browser game, crowd control game, funny combo game
  • ASO hook: “Can you read the room better than a comedian?”
  • SEM creative: side-by-side clips showing different crowd reactions to the same act

KPIs

  • Average stars per act
  • Act completion rate
  • Perfect-act percentage
  • Retry rate after meltdown losses

Experiments / Risks

  • Add rotating weekly crowd archetypes
  • Risk: balance must stay legible, not random
