Oracle Braid Uprising — Product Report

A pressure-logic game built from current signals around Connections-style grouping, daily brain teasers, and social shareability. Players reroute three prophecy streams through a living board while corruption climbs and combo windows tempt greedy fixes.

Strategy & Hook

The hook is legible in seconds: rotate glowing tiles until all colored streams reach matching shrines. The twist is that every rotation also stirs the corruption meter, making it feel less like a static pipe puzzle and more like a dramatic rescue sprint.

Trend Rationale

  • Work-market signals show durable puzzle appetite: Wordle, Connections, Strands.
  • Designed to feel clip-worthy through “saved it at 97% corruption” moments.
  • High replay value via board reshuffles and three-board runs.

Core Loop

  1. Read the board instantly: sources left, shrines right.
  2. Rotate tiles to complete all 3 color routes.
  3. Cash out points plus corruption-headroom bonus.
  4. Survive 3 escalating rounds to win the run.

Rewards & Progression

  • Score per restored route plus full-board bonus.
  • Badges: First Knot, Triple Seer, Uprising Ender.
  • Win condition: clear 3 boards.
  • Lose condition: corruption reaches 100%.

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • Keywords: rotate puzzle game, stream routing puzzle, daily logic challenge, browser brain game.
  • Ad angle: “Can you untangle the prophecy before the board revolts?”
  • Store angle: elegant logic under pressure, fast restarts, dramatic near-misses.

KPIs & Experiments

  • Time to first full clear.
  • Retry rate after sub-10% fails.
  • Average rotations per winning run.
  • Future tests: seeded daily braids, cursed tiles, limited-use rewind.

Risks

  • Needs larger topology pool for long-term depth.
  • Could add optional soft hints for weaker players.
