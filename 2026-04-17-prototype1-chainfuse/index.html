ChainFuse

Master the art of explosive chain reactions

⚡ How to Play

  1. Click anywhere on the field to place your ignition
  2. Watch the chain reaction cascade through the orbs
  3. Different colors explode in different patterns:
    • 🔴 Red: Explodes in + shape (4 directions)
    • 🔵 Blue: Explodes in X shape (diagonals)
    • 🟢 Green: Explodes in a circle (hits neighbors)
    • 🟡 Yellow: Explodes horizontally
    • 🟣 Purple: Explodes vertically
  4. Destroy at least 80% of orbs to advance
  5. Get 100% for a Perfect Clear bonus!
