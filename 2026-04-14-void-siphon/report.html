🎮 Game Concept & Hook

One-Sentence Hook: "Press-your-luck energy extraction - siphon unstable voids for power, but one collapse destroys everything."

Core Concept: Void Siphon combines the psychological tension of gambling with strategic resource management. Players face unstable energy pockets that can be siphoned for power, but each extraction risks catastrophic collapse. The "bank" mechanic lets players secure progress, but greed (continuing to siphon) unlocks combo multipliers.

Why It's Bold: This isn't just a "press your luck" game like Deal or No Deal. It adds strategic depth through:

  • Multiple simultaneous risk targets (5-7 voids, any can collapse)
  • Combo multipliers reward consecutive plays
  • Visual risk feedback (stability rings, pulsing warnings)
  • Meaningful decisions every single click

Unique Selling Points:

  • Real risk visualization - see exactly how unstable each void is
  • Combo system rewards aggressive play while punishing greed
  • All-or-nothing tension - any collapse = total loss
  • Session length flexibility - bank early for safety or push for S rank

🔄 Core Loop Explanation

The Greed Cycle

SAFE (Bank) RISKY (×2-3) DANGER (×4-5) DEATH

1. Assess (1 second): Player sees 5-7 void pockets with varying energy and stability levels. Risk meter shows current danger.

2. Decide (Continuous): Which void to siphon? Low stability = high risk but usually more energy. Safe voids = reliable but slower.

3. Siphon (Click): Extract energy. Watch stability ring decrease. Feel the tension as RNG determines fate.

4. React (Immediate): Success = energy gained + combo increased. Failure = total loss, game over.

5. Evaluate (Every siphon): Bank now and secure gains? Or push for higher multiplier?

6. Win/Lose (End state): Reach target energy = victory with S/A/B/C rank. Any collapse = lose all progress.

Session Length: 2-10 minutes | Core Emotion: Tension, greed, relief, regret

⚠️ Risk Factors

🔴 Ethical Concerns

Gambling-adjacent mechanics could attract regulatory scrutiny or trigger addiction in vulnerable players.

✅ Mitigation

Implement session limits, "responsible gaming" warnings, no real-money prizes. Position as skill puzzle, not gambling.

🔴 Frustration Churn

Losing everything to RNG feels terrible. Players may quit after first collapse.

✅ Mitigation

"Ghost mode" where players can practice risk-free. Stats page showing "near misses" to encourage retry.

🔴 Shallow Depth

Core mechanic is simple. Extended play may feel repetitive.

✅ Mitigation

Special void types (explosive, regenerating, chained). Unlockable modifiers. Daily challenges.

🔴 Balance Issues

Too easy = boring. Too hard = rage quit. RNG can create impossible or trivial runs.

✅ Mitigation

Extensive playtesting for bell curve distribution. Ensure 90% of runs are winnable with perfect play.
