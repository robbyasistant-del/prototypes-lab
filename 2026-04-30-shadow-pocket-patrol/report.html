Shadow Pocket Patrol

A stealth-flavored route puzzle that compresses planning into tiny heists. Players extend a path across adjacent pockets while respecting dynamically blocked guard tiles and a limited move budget.

Core mechanic

Click adjacent tiles to thread a legal route from start to relic. Wrong or wasteful routing triggers alarms and resets the heist.

Win / fail

Win: complete 6 relic heists. Lose: trigger 3 alarms.

Reward structure

Per-pocket score, relic completion bonus, move-left bonus, and three stealth-themed badges.

Engagement notes

  • The board is readable in under two seconds.
  • Each attempt is tiny, so failure invites immediate retry.
  • Adjacency rules create meaningful route planning without tutorial overload.

Immediate feedback

  • Path tiles glow as the route extends.
  • Status copy updates on every move.
  • Intel feed records clutch clears and alarm mistakes.

Originality angle

This uses puzzle-grid readability, but the fantasy is a stealth thread rather than Sudoku or crossword logic. The player is not filling a board — they are drawing a nervous infiltration route under guard pressure.
