Trend Weave — Product Report

A chain-crafting puzzle built from trend signals around Wordle/Connections/Strands plus fast social-content loops. Instead of solving static categories, players weave adjacent topic chains to fulfill live briefs before the board decays.

Concept & Hook

The player is a live trend-weaver. Every move forges a short narrative chain—meme, clip, fandom, drama, creator—creating the feeling of building momentum from chaos. It keeps the low-friction appeal of a word puzzle but adds routing pressure and combo greed.

Trend Rationale

  • Wordle / Connections / Strands were the clearest recent ranking signals.
  • X / YouTube / TikTok social-content signals point toward remixable, creator-facing loops.
  • The mechanic deliberately blends daily-think readability with social-virality fantasy.

Core Loop

  1. Read three live briefs immediately on screen.
  2. Click adjacent tiles to build a topic chain matching one brief recipe.
  3. Submit the chain for score, streak, decay recovery, and badges.
  4. Survive until all required briefs are cleared.

Scoring, Rewards, Progression

  • Base points per completed brief + bonus for longer exact chains
  • Combo multiplier for consecutive successful briefs
  • Badges: Pulse Starter, Trend Smith, Viral Architect
  • Win state: complete all 12 briefs
  • Game over: decay meter maxes out

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • SEO: trend puzzle game, adjacent chain game, browser strategy puzzle, viral weaving game
  • Ad angle: “Can you build a viral chain before the feed collapses?”
  • ASO angle: strategy puzzle with short sessions and daily scalability

KPIs

  • Time-to-first-clear
  • Brief completion rate
  • Average combo depth
  • Run restart rate after near-loss states

Experiments

  • Daily seeded boards
  • Theme packs per region
  • Powerups that freeze decay or wildcard a tile

Risks

  • Need more recipe variety for long-term retention
  • Adjacency hinting may need onboarding refinement on mobile
