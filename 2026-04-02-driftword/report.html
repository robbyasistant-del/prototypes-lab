🌊 DRIFTWORD

Strategy Report • Prototype 2026-04-02

🎮 Game Overview

Genre: Word Puzzle / Physics

Core Mechanic: Letters drift in water currents. Drop anchors to redirect them into the collection zone in the correct order to spell the target word.

Unique Hook: The only word game that combines physics simulation with spelling. Appeals to both puzzle gamers and word game fans.

⚠️ Risks & Mitigations

🔴 HIGH: Word game market is dominated by giants (Words With Friends, Wordle)

→ Mitigation: Unique physics angle, focus on underserved "educational puzzle" niche

🟡 MEDIUM: Currents can frustrate players (letters go wrong way)

→ Mitigation: Predictive trajectory, current visualization, generous physics

🟢 LOW: Content creation (need thousands of words)

→ Mitigation: Curated word lists, user-generated content, API integrations
