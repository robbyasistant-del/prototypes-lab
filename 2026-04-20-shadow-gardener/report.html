🌿 Shadow Gardener

Game Strategy, Monetization & Market Analysis

🎮 Game Hook (Elevator Pitch)

"Shadow Gardener" is a tranquil yet strategic puzzle game where you nurture bioluminescent plants by casting the perfect shadows. Light beams stream across your garden grid—too much light burns your plants, too little starves them. Place umbrellas, clouds, and mystical crystals to create the ideal gloom zones and watch your garden bloom.

The game combines ray-casting physics, grid-based strategy, and real-time growth simulation into a unique puzzle experience that feels like a digital bonsai garden crossed with light-bending optics.

🔄 Core Game Loop

  1. Observe: Players assess the light sources and plant positions on the grid
  2. Plan: Identify which plants need more/less shadow (shown in tooltips)
  3. Place: Drag shadow-casting objects (umbrellas, clouds, crystals) onto the grid
  4. Rotate: Adjust object orientation to optimize shadow coverage
  5. React: Watch real-time feedback—plants show health bars and states (burn/wilt/grow)
  6. Iterate: Add, remove, or reposition objects until all plants bloom
  7. Reward: Earn stars, unlock badges, progress to more complex levels

🎯 Key Mechanics

Ray-Casting Shadow System

Light sources cast rays across the grid. Shadow objects block these rays based on their shape and rotation. The system computes real-time shadow coverage per tile (0-100%).

Plant Growth Simulation

Each plant species has specific shadow needs (e.g., Moonflower: 35-55%). Plants grow when conditions are perfect, burn when over-lit, and wilt when under-lit.

Tool Variety

4 shadow object types with different sizes and blocking percentages: Umbrella (90%), Crystal (95%), Cloud (70%), Shade Panel (80%).

Scoring & Progression

3-star rating per level based on: bloom completion, time par, move efficiency. Badges reward mastery: Green Thumb, Minimalist, Speed Grower.

Summary

Shadow Gardener targets the underserved "relaxing but challenging" puzzle niche. Its unique light/shadow mechanic provides intellectual satisfaction while the bioluminescent aesthetic delivers emotional payoff. With careful monetization that respects the player's zen state, this game can capture both high retention and solid ARPDAU.

Key to success: Never compromise the tranquility for monetization pressure.
