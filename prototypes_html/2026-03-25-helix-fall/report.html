Helix Fall — Tower Spin Rework Report

Play the rotating-tower prototype

Why this rework happened

The previous rebuild improved readability but still framed play as moving the ball horizontally inside a shaft. That was the wrong feel. This pass changes the fantasy so the ball stays centered while the pillar and stacked platforms rotate under it, closer to Helix Jump.

What changed in r7 tower-spin

  • Centered player ball that remains visually anchored over the tower during play.
  • Rotating tower presentation driven by drag/swipe and keyboard input.
  • Angular platform sectors with safe cyan slices, dark gaps, and red danger wedges.
  • Side hiding / wraparound effect so platform slices visually move around the left and right sides of the pillar as you spin.
  • Natural controls that let the player drag the world rather than chase the ball.
  • Preserved game loop: rotate a gap beneath the ball to keep falling, bounce on safe slices, die on red.
  • Visible version bump to v2026.03.25-r7 tower-spin.

Design intent

This is still a lightweight 2D prototype, but the interaction model now matches the expected mental model much better. The player reads the ball as fixed and the tower as the object being manipulated. That single change makes the whole toy feel closer to the reference genre.

Local verification target

Before shipping, verify locally that: the ball remains centered, dragging right rotates the tower right, dragging left reverses it, platform slices visually wrap around the pillar edges, gaps and red wedges are readable, and the mirrored docs files match the prototype files.
