GRAVITY WEAVER

Physics Puzzle Game - Strategy Report

🎮 Game Concept & Hook

GRAVITY WEAVER is a physics-based puzzle game where players draw gravitational fields to guide particles through obstacle courses. The core innovation is the "Direct-Manipulation Physics" system - no rigid pre-placed tools, players create dynamic force fields in real-time.

The Hook: Unlike traditional physics puzzles with fixed elements, Gravity Weaver gives players god-like control over gravity itself. The "draw anywhere" mechanic creates emergent solutions where players discover their own approaches to each puzzle.

🔄 Core Loop Explanation

  • 1. Observe: Analyze particle trajectory and obstacle layout
  • 2. Plan: Consider optimal gravity field placement for energy efficiency
  • 3. Draw: Click and drag to create gravitational wells of varying strength
  • 4. Simulate: Watch particles react to gravity fields in real-time
  • 5. Iterate: Adjust fields or clear and retry for better efficiency
  • 6. Complete: Guide all particles to targets with energy remaining

Retention Mechanics

  • Star Rating: 3-star system encourages replay for perfect scores
  • Energy Constraint: Limited resource creates optimization puzzle layer
  • Progressive Complexity: 8 levels with escalating difficulty curve
  • Multiple Solutions: No single "correct" path encourages experimentation
  • Master Badge: End-game achievement for completionists
