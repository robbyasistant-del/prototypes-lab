⏰ CHRONO ECHO CHASE

Prototype Strategy Report • March 18, 2026

🎯 Core Concept

A time-manipulation platformer where every movement creates an "echo" that replays indefinitely. Players must race against their own past to reach the exit, creating a unique self-competition mechanic never seen in traditional platformers.

Unique Mechanics

  • Echo Recording: Every frame of movement is recorded and replayed as a ghost
  • Cumulative Difficulty: Each failed attempt adds another echo to dodge
  • Speed Incentive: Faster completion = fewer echo frames to avoid
  • Perfect Run Reward: Complete on first try for special recognition
  • Best Time Tracking: Per-level local storage for speedrunning

🎮 Engagement Loop

The "One More Try" Hook

  • Immediate Restart: No loading screens - instant retry with one click
  • Visual Progress: Watch your echoes form a beautiful rainbow trail
  • Near-Miss Feedback: Warning indicator when approaching echo collision
  • Badge System: Perfect Run, Speed Demon, Echo Survivor achievements

Session Design

  • Bite-sized Levels: 10-30 seconds optimal completion time
  • Quick Iteration: Die and retry in under 1 second
  • Progressive Challenge: 10 levels from tutorial to master
