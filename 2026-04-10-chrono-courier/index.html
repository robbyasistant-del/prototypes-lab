How to play
Tap adjacent tiles to draw a route from the courier. Pick up the glowing requested parcels, avoid anomalies, then hit Run Route. You need 3 successful deliveries to win. If stability hits 0 or the route is invalid, the shift ends.
Juicy bit: every route is a mini heist. Over-plan and you burn energy. Under-plan and you miss the combo.
