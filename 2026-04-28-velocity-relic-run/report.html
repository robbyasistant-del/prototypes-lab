Velocity Relic Run — Product Report

A greed-driven survival sprint that mixes the readability of lane action with clicker-style banking tension. The fresh twist is the explicit decision to lock your stash before a meltdown wipes it.

Concept & Hook

Players collect unstable relic shards during fast lane dashes, then choose when to bank them at vault gates. Greed raises score potential and danger simultaneously.

Trend Rationale

  • Endless runner and viral clicker both ranked high in recent prediction signals.
  • Browser casual demand remains strong, especially around low-friction repeat loops.
  • This prototype fuses runner readability with greed-based stash decisions rather than endless avoidance only.

Core Loop

  1. Dash through lanes to collect relic shards and dodge hazards.
  2. Build an unbanked stash multiplier.
  3. Hit a vault gate to secure points before meltdown.
  4. Reach the target score or survive until the extraction round.

Scoring / Rewards / States

  • Shard pickups increase stash and score potential
  • Bank actions convert stash into locked score
  • Badges: Greed Spark, Cool Banker, Meltdown Master
  • Win state: target score reached or final extraction survived
  • Game over: health depleted

Difficulty Tuning

  • Scroll speed ramps gradually
  • Hazard frequency rises when stash is high
  • Vault windows create satisfying risk/reward peaks

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • SEO terms: relic runner game, bank your score game, lane heist browser game
  • Paid hook: “Grab more or bank now? One greed move can melt the run.”
  • Thumbnail concept: glowing stash meter on the edge of overload

KPIs / Experiments / Risks

  • Bank usage rate before first death
  • Average stash lost to greed
  • Restart intent after near-miss meltdowns
  • Experiment with daily relic mutators and special vaults
  • Risk: balance must reward bravery without feeling punishing
