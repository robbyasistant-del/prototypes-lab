Word Arrow Escape

WORD + SPEED • pick the correct arrow to build each word letter-by-letter.
How to play
- Your current letter is in the center.
- The 4 arrows (up/right/down/left) show candidate next letters.
- Press the direction that matches the next required letter.
- Finish each word before the timer ends.

Immediate feedback: green = correct, red = mistake.
Lives: 3. Wrong arrow or timeout costs 1 life.
Win: Clear all 8 words. Game Over: 0 lives.
