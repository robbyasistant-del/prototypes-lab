Comet Tag Forge — detailed report

Genre: Slingshot action puzzle
Signal: trick-shot satisfaction + creator-friendly spectacle + quick retry loops
Date: 2026-04-04

Strategy

This prototype chases highly watchable “one clean shot” satisfaction. The concept is bold but easy to parse: the player is forging murals by launching paint comets into moving-looking stencil targets.

Loop

  • Drag to aim, fire a comet.
  • Watch the arc and instantly know if the shot was good.
  • Complete a mural set before energy runs out.
  • Advance through 5 murals for the win state.

Rewards

Strong shot feedback, score spikes, level clears, and badge tiers: Spark Rookie, Trail Blazer, Graffiti Ace, Meteor Idol.

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • SEO: slingshot puzzle game, trick shot arcade, comet paint game, target aim challenge.
  • ASO subtitle: “Launch comets. Finish the mural.”
  • SEM creative: supercut of impossible hits, perfect-arc fails, and last-energy clutch clears.

KPIs

  • First-shot-under-8-seconds rate: 80%+
  • Same-session retry rate: 52%+
  • Mural 3 reach: 58%+
  • Clutch-win share/save rate: 9%+

Experiments

  1. Wind gust modifier vs pure arc shots.
  2. Moving stencil targets on late murals.
  3. Charge shot visualizer to reduce aim confusion.

Risks

  • Projectile feel needs further tuning to land exactly between forgiving and skillful.
  • Without richer end-level splashes, long-term reward fantasy may flatten.
  • Touch precision on smaller phones should be tested aggressively.
