🌀 MOMENTUM MOSAIC

World Rotation Physics Game

🎯 Concept Overview

MOMENTUM MOSAIC is a physics-based puzzle game where players rotate the entire game world to control gravity's direction. Set in a circular kaleidoscope, players guide a glowing orb through dynamic mazes, collecting crystalline shards while avoiding deadly obstacles.

Unique Selling Point: Unlike typical gravity-switching games, the entire world rotates around the player, creating a hypnotic kaleidoscope effect. The physics simulation requires players to anticipate momentum and trajectory in a constantly shifting reference frame.

Core Mechanics

  • World Rotation: Arrow keys or A/D rotate the entire mosaic
  • Relative Gravity: Gravity always pulls toward the "bottom" of the rotated world
  • Momentum Physics: The orb maintains velocity and reacts realistically to forces
  • Boost Ability: Limited-use speed boost for tight situations
  • Circular Arena: Bounce off outer walls or face elimination

🎮 Gameplay Loop

  1. ASSESS - Observe shard positions and obstacle layout
  2. ROTATE - Turn the world to create favorable gravity angles
  3. SLING - Use momentum to arc toward targets
  4. ADJUST - Fine-tune rotation mid-flight
  5. COLLECT - Gather all shards to complete the mosaic
  6. ESCAPE - Avoid red obstacles or fall into the void

Progression System

  • Level 1: Simple triangle shard formation, no obstacles
  • Level 2: Central obstacle blocks direct paths
  • Level 3: Multiple obstacles requiring precise navigation
  • Level 4+: Procedurally generated mazes with increasing complexity
