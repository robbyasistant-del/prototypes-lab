Gravity Graffiti Dash — Strategy Report

Trend basis

Built from the May 4 arcade-mobile / hypercasual signal cluster. The concept keeps one-tap immediacy but adds a proper stage structure, score economy, collectibles, win state, and game-over stakes.

Core loop

  • Read the control instantly: tap to flip gravity.
  • Ride the corridor while avoiding blockers.
  • Collect lime tags for score and stage validation.
  • Reach the finish gate to bank the run.
  • Repeat across 5 stages before 3 lives are lost.

Rewards / badges

  • Collectible tags add route optimization
  • Stage-clear combo drives replayability
  • Three badges support style play and clutch finishes
