Rumor Rodeo — Product Report

A social-chaos reaction game tuned to viral content signals from X, Reddit, YouTube, and TikTok. Players triage exploding gossip packets into Believe, Doubt, or Bury lanes before crowd trust collapses.

Strategy & Hook

The game weaponizes internet instinct. In two seconds, players understand the fantasy: decide what gets amplified, questioned, or silenced. The speed and combo system produce an immediate “one more try” urge.

Trend Rationale

  • Latest content signals are social-platform heavy, so the theme leans into moderation, virality, and meme-speed decision making.
  • Built for reactive play, score-chasing, and streak drama.
  • Feels contemporary without depending on external APIs.

Core Loop

  1. Read the incoming rumor card.
  2. Send it left, center, or right into the correct lane.
  3. Build trust streaks, trigger multipliers, survive escalating speeds.
  4. Reach the target score before trust breaks.

Rewards & Progression

  • Points per correct triage plus streak multiplier.
  • Badges: Fact Checker, Crowd Wrangler, Rodeo Legend.
  • Win condition: reach 2400 trust points.
  • Lose condition: trust falls to zero.

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • Keywords: reaction sorting game, social media chaos game, viral moderation browser game.
  • Ad angle: “Could you survive one minute as the internet's rumor sheriff?”
  • Store angle: funny, fast, topical, highly replayable.

KPIs & Experiments

  • Accuracy by lane.
  • Average streak length.
  • Retry rate after near-target losses.
  • Future tests: daily decks, influencer boss rounds, versus hot-seat mode.

Risks

  • Needs more content packs for long-term freshness.
  • Difficulty spikes must remain readable on mobile.
