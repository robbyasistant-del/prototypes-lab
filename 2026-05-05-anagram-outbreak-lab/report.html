Anagram Outbreak Lab — Product Report

A word-pressure prototype rooted in current word-game and anagram interest. The player recombines unstable letter cultures into valid antidote words while infection spreads across a petri board.

Strategy & Hook

The hook is tactile discovery under threat. Instead of leisurely unscrambling, each turn is a race to spot a meaningful word before nearby cells corrupt. That keeps it distinct from slower static word puzzlers.

Trend rationale

  • Fresh signals: word games, anagram game, wordle-adjacent daily behavior.
  • Combines familiar word joy with visible escalating stakes.
  • Strong social proof angle through “I saved the lab with a 6-letter word” moments.

Core loop

  1. Read the target hint immediately.
  2. Tap letters to build a word matching the hint family.
  3. Submit valid words to purge infected cells and score.
  4. Clear enough vials before infection overruns the lab.

Scoring, rewards, progression

  • Points scale with word length and streak.
  • Longer words purge more infection and unlock badges.
  • Badges: First Cure, Deep Lexicon, Lab Savior.
  • Win: clear 8 vials.
  • Game over: infection reaches 100%.

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • Keywords: anagram game, word puzzle browser, daily word challenge, letter strategy game.
  • Ad angle: “Can you cure the outbreak with your vocabulary?”
  • ASO angle: word game with urgency, streaks, and satisfying board feedback.

KPIs & Experiments

  • Average valid word length.
  • Hint completion rate.
  • Restarts after 80% infection state.
  • Future tests: daily seeded dictionary, lightning mode, hard mode with decoys.

Risks

  • Dictionary size needs expansion for depth.
  • Hint clarity matters to avoid brute-force feeling.
