Pips Plunder Parade - Product Report

Strategy

Leans into Pips discovery while avoiding simple dice matching. The exact-sum shrine fantasy adds tension, readable goals, and a crunchy “just one better roll” urge that suits browser/mobile sessions.

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • SEO: dice puzzle browser game, pips style game online, exact sum strategy game
  • ASO: “Feed the shrines exact pips before the vault burns.”
  • SEM angle: short ad showing an almost-perfect shrine overshoot causing instant chaos.

Core Loop

  • Read three exact-sum shrine goals
  • Draft rolled dice into those targets
  • Manage rerolls vs. heat pressure
  • Clear rounds and stack exact-hit combos
  • Push for a flawless 5-round vault run

Rewards

  • Immediate gain on exact matches
  • Combo growth for consecutive shrine clears
  • Badges: Exact Touch, Cool Hands, Vault Duke
  • Persistent best score

KPIs

  • Average rerolls per session
  • Exact-hit ratio
  • Bust rate per round
  • Replay rate after round-4 losses

Experiments

  • Character-specific dice with asymmetric faces
  • Daily seeded shrine patterns
  • Boss shrines with reward pickups after clears

Risks

  • Could benefit from a clearer tutorial nudge on exact totals for first-time players
  • Later balancing may need more recovery options after one bust
