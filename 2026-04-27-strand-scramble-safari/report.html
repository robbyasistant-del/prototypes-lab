Strand Scramble Safari - Product Report

Strategy

Takes Strands/Wordle momentum and turns it into a faster, path-tracing browser loop with stronger urgency. Theme-based words keep recognition high, while adjacency rules add tactile satisfaction and mastery.

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • SEO: word path puzzle, strands style browser game, trace letters online
  • ASO: “Trace wild word trails before dusk.”
  • SEM angle: side-by-side clip of a perfect chain vs. dusk filling after one bad move.

Core Loop

  • Scan one themed grid
  • Trace adjacent letters into live target words
  • Manage dusk and mistakes
  • Clear all targets for round bonus and badges
  • Chase a perfect 4-round win

Rewards

  • Immediate score per valid path extension
  • Bigger burst on full word completion
  • Badges: Clean Trail, Dusk Dodger, Safari Master
  • Persistent best score for replay pull

KPIs

  • Average mistakes per round
  • Drop-off before round 2
  • Replay rate after 75%+ dusk losses
  • Average time to first word completion

Experiments

  • Daily seed with shareable grids
  • Spanish word packs for ES traffic
  • Special hidden bonus word per round

Risks

  • Without more grid variety, expert players may memorize patterns
  • Could use audio cues in v2 to amplify path satisfaction
