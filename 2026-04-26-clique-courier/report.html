Clique Courier - Product Report

Strategy

Inspired by grouping-game momentum and meme-social archetypes. Instead of static deduction, the player feels like a frazzled but stylish courier routing social energy under pressure, which adds urgency, animation, and a clear replay loop.

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • SEO: social sorting game, connections style game online, fast category puzzle
  • ASO: “Can you read the vibe before the hallway jams?”
  • SEM angle: creative showing one parcel sent to the wrong clique causing comic chaos.

Core Loop

  • Scan clue tags on a parcel
  • Map it to one of four hidden cliques
  • Route it fast to prevent jam growth
  • Clear a wave and earn stars based on cleanliness
  • Push for 10+ stars across 4 waves

Rewards

  • Immediate score gain/loss per route
  • Wave bonus based on low-jam efficiency
  • Three badges: Clean Gossip, Jam Dodger, Clique Legend
  • Persistent high score

KPIs

  • Misroute rate by wave
  • Average jam level when a player quits
  • Star yield distribution
  • Replay rate after wave 3 losses

Experiments

  • Daily seeded archetype packs
  • Timed rival mode where AI courier steals points
  • Voiceover or meme sticker reward layers

Risks

  • Clue clarity must remain readable on mobile
  • Needs fresh archetypes over time to avoid memorization fatigue
