Celestial Garden Raid — Report

Prototype date: 2026-04-09

VALIDATION

Checked:

  • index.html exists and loads as a self-contained prototype
  • instructions, score, and goal state are visible from the first screen
  • game has complete loop, badge rewards, explicit win / lose states, and immediate per-action feedback
  • mechanic is distinct from strands / connections clones: this is a spatial chain-reaction harvesting planner with mood propagation and exact bundle delivery

Concept

A magical-garden raid puzzle where the player chooses where to start a harvest cascade. Creature moods and bloom types propagate differently, so planning the opening tap is the whole delicious problem.

Why it can hook

The board looks rich immediately, the rules are readable in seconds, and every click creates a visible, juicy payoff. Players will want the perfect ritual-bundle run after seeing how a slightly better opening could have solved the whole board.

Strategy

This should be framed as cozy on the surface, ruthless under the hood. Strong visual appeal for ads, but the retention angle comes from exact-bundle planning rather than generic match mechanics.

SEO / ASO / SEM

cozy puzzle gamechain reaction strategygarden puzzledaily ritual gamecasual fantasy puzzle

Ad hook: show one opening tap causing a huge mood cascade and a full bundle completion.

Core loop

  1. Read the exact ritual bundle request.
  2. Study creature moods and bloom spreads.
  3. Trigger one plot and watch the cascade.
  4. Adapt next raids to avoid overflow.
  5. Win with exact delivery or lose to nightfall / over-collection.

Rewards

Short-session satisfaction, badge collection, improved board-reading mastery, and strong visual delight from chain reactions.

KPIs

  • session restarts after first loss
  • bundle completion rate
  • average over-collection penalty per run
  • run duration
  • badge rate by day 3

Experiments

  • Daily garden seed
  • Night modifiers that alter creature mood rules
  • Meta unlockable creatures
  • Mobile haptic emphasis on big cascades

Risks

  • Needs carefully signposted cascade logic for mainstream users
  • Too much chain opacity would reduce perceived fairness
  • Late content must add meaningful creature interactions, not just more tiles
