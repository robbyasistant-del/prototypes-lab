Neon Oracle Switchboard — Report

Prototype date: 2026-04-09

VALIDATION

Checked:

  • index.html exists and opens as a self-contained game
  • instructions are visible immediately in the HUD
  • game loop includes score, badges, explicit win / game-over states, and instant action feedback
  • mechanic is distinct from pure word / connections clones: it is a deduction-routing switchboard puzzle with cascading prophecy constraints

Concept

A neon occult switchboard puzzle where each move reroutes signal colors through a prophecy grid. The player is not solving words; they are satisfying evolving network constraints under pressure and chasing perfect multi-route turns.

Why it can hook

It mixes low-friction click-and-understand onboarding with the brain-satisfaction of deduction plus the juiciness of chain routing. Good players instantly see how they could squeeze one cleaner board out of the same seed — very strong one-more-try energy.

Strategy

Best positioned as a premium-feeling daily puzzle arcade hybrid: deduction with spectacle. The strongest angle is not another daily word game, but a switchboard ritual puzzle that feels alive.

SEO / ASO / SEM

daily puzzle gamelogic routing gamesignal puzzleneon brain gameshort session mobile puzzle

Creative angle: Route prophecies, not pipes. Paid UA should test ultra-short clips that show one huge chain-completion turn in under 8 seconds.

Core loop

  1. Read prophecy constraints.
  2. Rotate / route the board.
  3. Trigger satisfying signal beams and score bursts.
  4. Resolve round as win or overload game over.
  5. Earn badges and replay for cleaner routing.

Rewards

Short-run score chase, performance badges, better mastery of forecasting board states, and potential daily streak framing.

KPIs

  • D1 retention on first 3 sessions
  • average runs per session
  • time-to-first-restart after a loss
  • perfect-board completion rate
  • badge earn rate by session 5

Experiments

  • Daily fixed seed vs endless mode
  • Stronger prophecy mutation every 3 turns
  • Meta rewards for clean-routing streaks
  • Alternative input: drag wires vs rotate nodes

Risks

  • If late-game board states feel opaque, cognitive load may spike too hard
  • Needs careful balancing so losses feel fair, not random
  • Visual readability is critical when many beams animate together
