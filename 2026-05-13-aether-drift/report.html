Aether Drift

Prototype Report — May 13, 2026

🎯 Strategy

Concept: Momentum-based physics navigation game where players control a luminous particle through space using Newtonian pulse propulsion. The unique mechanic — clicking propels you in the OPPOSITE direction — creates a skillful, meditative-yet-intense gameplay loop that differentiates from typical tap-to-fly games.

Target Audience

Casual mobile gamers aged 18-35 who enjoy "one more try" games with high skill ceilings. Appeals to fans of Flappy Bird, Geometry Dash, and Dune! — but with a physics-driven twist.

Monetization Potential

  • Ad-supported: Rewarded video for extra lives (max 1 per level). Interstitial between levels.
  • Cosmetics IAP: Particle skins (fire, ice, neon, bio), trail effects, gate themes.
  • Season Pass: Monthly "Nebula Tours" with 30 hand-crafted levels + exclusive cosmetics.
  • Premium: $2.99 remove-ads with bonus "Void Walker" skin.

🔄 Core Loop

LOAD LEVEL

PULSE to navigate → Build momentum → Thread the needle through gates
    ↓
COLLECT orbs for score → Maintain combo streak for multiplier
    ↓
AVOID void zones (instant death) → Wall bounces break combo
    ↓
ALL GATES PASSED? ──YES──→ LEVEL COMPLETE → Badge evaluation → Next Level
         │
         └─NO──→ Continue drifting (time penalty) or Die (lose life)
    ↓
3 LIVES LOST → GAME OVER → Share score → Retry → Back to Menu

Session target: 3-7 minutes for full 10-level run. Individual level: 15-45 seconds.
