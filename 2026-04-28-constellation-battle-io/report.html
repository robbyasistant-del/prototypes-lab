Constellation Battle IO — Product Report

A single-player adaptation of the IO battle feeling: instead of directly eating or shooting, the player draws star territory under threat while hunters actively sever fragile links.

Concept & Hook

The loop turns spatial planning into survival drama. Every line you draw increases score and map control but also creates a vulnerable line for hunters to cut.

Trend Rationale

  • IO battle royale scored highly in the latest prediction set.
  • Browser-game and casual-game queries remain strong in recent signals.
  • The design keeps the instantly legible “claim space / survive pressure” fantasy without cloning legacy IO formulas.

Core Loop

  1. Click safe drifting beacons to connect them in sequence.
  2. Close triangles to claim sectors and earn burst points.
  3. Avoid hunters crossing the link path.
  4. Reach target score before lives run out.

Scoring & Rewards

  • Each beacon connected: points
  • Closed triangles: large territory bonus
  • Badges: First Chain, Triangle Taker, Nebula Survivor
  • Win state: hit the target constellation score
  • Game over: all hull lives lost

Difficulty Tuning

  • Hunters speed up slowly over time
  • More drifting beacons increase opportunity and risk
  • Missed clicks are forgiven; bad route timing is the real pressure point

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • SEO terms: constellation game online, territory survival browser game, star linking game
  • Paid angle: “Draw star routes before hunters cut the line.”
  • Social hook: beautiful starfield + clean fail moments work well in clips

KPIs

  • Average chain length per run
  • Triangle completion rate
  • Restarts per session
  • Target score conversion

Experiments & Risks

  • Add ranked seed maps or daily skies
  • Add powerups that bend hunter routes
  • Risk: tutorial needs to show triangle-claiming clearly
