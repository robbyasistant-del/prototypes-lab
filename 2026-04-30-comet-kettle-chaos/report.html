Comet Kettle Chaos

This prototype turns casual drag-and-drop into a frantic celestial kitchen. The player processes moving ingredients from a conveyor-belt sky into recipe-specific cauldrons while racing spills and time.

Core mechanic

Drag randomly spawning ingredients into the correct pot matching the active order’s two-step recipe.

Win / fail

Win: complete 10 orders in 120 seconds. Lose: hit 4 spills or expire with fewer than 10 orders.

Rewards

Per-ingredient points, full-order bonuses, fast-delivery streaks, and three named badges.

Why it feels replayable

  • Recipe queue forces prioritization under pressure.
  • Missed ingredients matter, so each drag has stakes.
  • Fast-delivery badge nudges mastery and cleaner routing.

Feedback systems

  • Pot fill levels visually grow as correct drops land.
  • Kitchen chatter log confirms every success and mistake instantly.
  • Spill counter and order queue make the danger legible at all times.

Originality angle

Instead of a restaurant clone, this leans into arcade potion management with a moving-space-belt and strict two-ingredient recipe logic. It reads instantly but becomes delightfully hectic.
