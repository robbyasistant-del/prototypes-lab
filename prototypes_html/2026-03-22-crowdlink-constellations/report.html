Crowdlink Constellations — Strategy Report

Concept

A bold sports-daily riff inspired by the strong retention signal around Connections Sports Edition. The twist: category discovery becomes tactile star-drafting instead of a flat list.

Core loop

  • Read eight clue-stars and infer the hidden sports families.
  • Select exactly four clues and lock the constellation.
  • Preserve crowd energy while chasing first-try solves.
  • Clear four rounds before 3 strikes.

Reward design

  • High first-try score payout
  • Badge unlocks for clean play and calm runs
  • Immediate visual feedback via glowing links and round-clear momentum

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • Keywords: sports connections game, category puzzle, daily sports puzzle, clue grouping game
  • Store angle: “Sports Connections, but cinematic and tactile.”
  • SEM hook: “Find the sports pattern. Draw the constellation. Save the crowd.”

KPIs / experiments / risks

  • Round-1 solve rate
  • First-try solve rate by category type
  • Try daily seeded packs and leaderboards
  • Risk: needs a growing clue bank to sustain daily freshness
