Strategy Report • Prototype 2026-04-02

🎮 Game Overview

Genre: Puzzle / Physics / Color Mixing

Core Mechanic: Drop colored blobs that merge when they touch. Same colors combine and grow; different colors mix to create new colors. Strategic placement creates satisfying cascade chains.

Unique Hook: Combines the satisfaction of bubble merge games with color theory education. "Accidentally" learn color mixing while having fun.
