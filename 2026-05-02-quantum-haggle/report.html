⚛️ Quantum Haggle

2026-05-02 | Entanglement Trading Game | Strategy Report

🎯 Core Concept

A quantum physics-inspired trading game where particles are entangled. When you buy or sell one particle, its entangled partner reacts instantly — creating chain reactions you can exploit for profit. A bold, original mechanic that has NEVER been done in previous prototypes.

🎮 Gameplay Loop

  • Round starts with 8–12 quantum particles, some entangled in pairs
  • Player clicks a particle to select it
  • Buy when price is low, sell when price spikes
  • Trading one particle instantly changes its entangled partner's price
  • Chain reactions = bonus multipliers
  • Hit profit target within 60 seconds to advance to next round

🧠 Unique Mechanics

Quantum Entanglement

Each particle card shows its entangled partner. Buying particle A causes particle B's price to spike. Selling causes it to drop. This creates a strategic layer where you must plan 2 moves ahead.

Market Volatility

Prices oscillate with random walk + volatility per particle type. Photon is stable; Tachyon is wild. Risk/reward profiles vary.

Time Pressure

Timer bar shifts from cyan → yellow → red. Late-round pressure forces snap decisions.
