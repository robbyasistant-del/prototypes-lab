🎯 Game Hook

"Steal the sound. Escape the silence."

Resonance Vault is a sound-stealth puzzle game where players infiltrate a music-themed vault hidden in total darkness. The twist: your only way to see is to make noise. Sonar pulses reveal the map, guards, and treasure — but each pulse is a gamble. Ambient noise (dripping water, wind, thunder) creates rhythm-based safe windows. Time your pulses. Read the guards. Vanish with the loot.

The game fuses three dopamine loops:

  • Exploration tension — "What's behind that wall?"
  • Rhythm mastery — nailing a perfect pulse during a thunderclap
  • Stealth perfection — completing a level undetected earns the "Ghost" badge
Why it stands out: Most stealth games punish visibility. Resonance Vault punishes sound. The sonar mechanic turns the player's primary tool into their greatest risk. The ambient noise meter adds a rhythm-game layer that no pure stealth or puzzle game offers.

🔄 Core Loop

1. Observe

Player studies the ambient noise meter. Green safe zones drift across the timeline. Guards patrol with visible (post-pulse) vision cones. Loot glitters in the dark.

2. Pulse

Spacebar or click emits a sonar ripple. If timed in a green zone, the pulse is silent. If red-zone, nearby guards become suspicious and converge on the source.

3. Move

Click or arrow keys to move one tile. Navigate revealed corridors. Avoid guard vision cones. Collect loot for score multipliers.

4. Escape

Reach the glowing EXIT tile. Time bonus ticks down — faster = higher score. Fewer pulses = stealth bonus. 100% loot = Looter King badge.

Progression Systems

  • 5 Levels — procedurally varied layouts, increasing guard count, tighter safe windows, more loot
  • 3 Badges per level — Ghost (0 detected pulses), Looter King (100% loot), Speed Thief (under par time)
  • Cumulative Score — loot value + time remaining × 10 + (total loot − detected pulses) × 50
  • Star Rating — 1★ clear, 2★ +1 badge, 3★ all badges

💰 Monetization Hooks

Tier 1 — Ad-Supported (Hypercasual funnel)

  • Interstitial ads between levels (every 2-3 levels to avoid fatigue)
  • Rewarded video → +15 seconds on failed level, or reveal full map for 3 seconds
  • Banner ad on game-over screen + main menu

Tier 2 — IAP / Premium ($2.99 unlock)

  • Remove all ads — one-time purchase
  • Sound Packs — alternative ambient themes ("Jazz Club", "Cyberpunk", "Rainforest") — $0.99 each
  • Thief Skins — visual player customizations — $0.99 each or $3.99 bundle
  • Endless Mode — infinite procedural levels, global leaderboard — $1.99

Tier 3 — Subscription ($4.99/mo "Vault Pass")

  • Daily challenge levels with unique modifiers
  • Exclusive skins and sound packs
  • Advanced analytics: heatmap of where you were detected most
  • "Ghost Run" replays — share undetected runs as GIFs
Conversion anchor: Show the player their "Ghost" badge progress during the game-over screen. "You were detected 1 time — remove ads and get unlimited retries to nail that perfect run." Scarcity + pride = conversion.

⚠️ Risk Assessment

RiskProbabilityImpactMitigation
High early churn
Players don't understand the sonar + noise mechanic		 HIGH HIGH Interactive tutorial (not just text). First level has oversized safe windows. Tooltip hints on first 3 pulses.
Difficulty spike
Level 3+ feels unfair due to guard AI		 MEDIUM HIGH Dynamic difficulty: if player dies 3x on same level, increase safe window duration by 20%. Cap guard speed.
Ad fatigue
Too many interstitials hurt retention		 MEDIUM MEDIUM Show interstitials only every 3 levels max. Prioritize rewarded video (opt-in). Offer ad-removal IAP prominently.
Genre confusion
Marketed as stealth but plays like puzzle		 LOW MEDIUM Store screenshots show both stealth (guards, vision cones) and puzzle (noise meter, timing). Tag both genres.
Session length too short
Full run is only ~10 min		 MEDIUM MEDIUM Add endless mode, daily challenges, and speedrun leaderboards. Badge hunting adds replay value.
Accessibility
Colorblind players can't see green/red zones		 LOW LOW Add patterns to safe zones (stripes). Include high-contrast mode. Provide audio cues for safe windows.
Clone risk
Mechanic is novel but copyable		 HIGH MEDIUM Build brand around "Resonance Vault" art style (dark vault + neon sonar). Add narrative hooks (lore fragments). Community features (shared ghost runs).
Platform dependency
Mobile web vs. native app performance		 MEDIUM LOW Canvas 2D is lightweight. Target 60fps on mid-tier devices. Test on iPhone 12 / Pixel 6 minimum. Consider Capacitor wrapper for native stores.
