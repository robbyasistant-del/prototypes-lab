📊 Executive Summary

ChainFuse is a high-engagement chain reaction puzzle game where players strategically place a single ignition point to trigger cascading explosions through a field of colored orbs. Each color features unique explosion patterns, creating depth and strategic replayability.

The game combines the satisfying feedback of explosive chain reactions with strategic puzzle elements, targeting casual gamers seeking quick, engaging play sessions (2-5 minutes per level).

Orb Type Symbol Explosion Pattern Strategic Use

🟣 Purple ↓ Vertical line Column clearing

Progression System

  • 8 Progressive Levels - Increasing difficulty, more orb types, obstacles
  • Level 1-2: Tutorial - Basic Red/Green orbs
  • Level 3-4: Intermediate - All 5 colors, obstacles introduced
  • Level 5-6: Advanced - Higher targets, more obstacles
  • Level 7-8: Expert - Maximum challenge, chaos patterns

⚠️ Risk Analysis

Risk Probability Impact Mitigation
User churn after Level 1 Medium High Ensure first level has satisfying chain; add skip tutorial option
Performance issues on mobile Medium Medium Optimize particle count; reduce canvas resolution on mobile
Difficulty spike perceived as unfair Medium High Gradual difficulty curve; hint system for optimal shots
Sound autoplay blocked by browsers High Low Visual feedback compensates; add manual audio toggle
Short gameplay lifespan Medium Medium Plan DLC levels; add daily challenges; infinite mode
Competition from similar games High Medium Unique visual style; social sharing of perfect clears

🚀 Monetization Strategy

Phase 1: Engagement (Current)

  • Free-to-play with all 8 levels unlocked
  • No ads in core gameplay
  • Focus on building user base and metrics

Phase 2: Retention (Month 2-3)

  • Add "Daily Challenge" - one curated level per day
  • Global leaderboards for total score
  • Cosmetic orb skins (unlockable via perfect clears)

Phase 3: Revenue (Month 4+)

  • Level Packs: $0.99 for 10 new levels
  • Undo Shot: $1.99 to remove ads + 1 undo per level
  • Premium Mode: $4.99 unlimited play + exclusive levels
  • Optional rewarded ads for hints/retry bonuses
