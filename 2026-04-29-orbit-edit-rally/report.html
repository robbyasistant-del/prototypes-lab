Orbit Edit Rally — Product Report

An action-arcade prototype inspired by video/social trend signals. The player is not simply dodging; they are performing live orbital edits, switching rings to catch raw media packets and banking them through matching gates for combo-fueled score bursts.

Concept & Hook

It delivers the fantasy of editing at live-stream speed. You are juggling three orbit lanes around a reactor, timing captures and deposits as the system speeds up. The loop is instantly readable and highly replayable.

Trend Rationale

  • Recent content signals emphasized X, YouTube, TikTok and creator-facing virality.
  • The mechanic turns content-flow language—clip, hook, stream, remix—into an arcade action loop.
  • Low-friction, score-chasing play fits browser casual habits.

Core Loop

  1. Switch between three orbit rings.
  2. Catch packets matching the active gate color.
  3. Bank packets through the correct gate to score and build combo.
  4. Survive escalating instability until target score is reached.

Scoring & Rewards

  • Each safe catch adds stash value
  • Banking converts stash to score with combo multipliers
  • Badges: First Cut, Triple Orbit, Reactor Hero
  • Win state: target score reached
  • Game over: reactor integrity falls to zero

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • SEO: arcade orbit game, editor speed game, browser reflex game, sci-fi score chaser
  • Ad angle: “Cut clips at orbital speed before the reactor blows.”
  • Social clip angle: high-clarity near-miss and combo moments

KPIs

  • Time to first successful bank
  • Average stash size before banking
  • Retry rate after reactor failures
  • Score distribution by session length

Experiments / Risks

  • Add daily orbital layouts or rotating mutators
  • Introduce special packets that temporarily freeze the reactor
  • Risk: color readability must remain clear on small screens
