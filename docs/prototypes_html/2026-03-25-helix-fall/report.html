Helix Fall Sprint — Sprint 1 Report

Play the prototype

Concept

A 2D vertical-fall browser prototype inspired by Helix Jump, translated into a side-on falling lane. The player steers a falling orb horizontally to pass through platform gaps while avoiding red danger bands and deciding when to land safely versus greedily chaining more drops.

What sprint 1 proves

  • Playable core loop in a browser, no dependencies.
  • Helix-like essence preserved in 2D: fall, thread gaps, timing, danger, progression, survival pressure.
  • Score and combo system rewarding chained drops.
  • Settings and persistent stats panel in the spirit of the 100 Jumps Clone II prototype.
  • Visible version/footer for easy iteration tracking.

Design notes

  • Danger state: a panic meter rises with speed; red bands punish lazy landings.
  • Risk/reward: safe landings reset combo, while gap chains produce better score and stronger feel.
  • Clean scope: one polished survival mode instead of too many half-baked modes.

Next obvious upgrades

  • Breakable floors and “smash through” power state after long chains.
  • Daily seed / challenge layouts.
  • More enemy-like floor patterns and moving hazards.
  • Audio pass and stronger progression unlocks.
