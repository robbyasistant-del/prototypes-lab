2026-04-30 prototype report

Neon Sigil Forge

A high-clarity orbit word-action prototype that mixes daily-word energy with arcade pressure. The player forges five clue-driven sigils by clicking letters on a rotating ring before stability or time runs out.

Core mechanic

Click orbiting letter shards in exact order to complete a target word. Wrong picks instantly punish stability and reset combo tension.

Why it hooks

Every round is one more try: the clue is understandable in seconds, the rotating board adds urgency, and perfect rounds snowball into visible combo rewards.

Win / fail

Win: forge 5 sigils. Lose: hit 0 stability or 0 time.

Scoring & rewards

  • Base score per forged word plus remaining time bonus.
  • Perfect round bonus for no mistakes.
  • Combo multiplier that scales consecutive clean rounds.
  • Badges: Precision Crafter, Combo Voltage, Glass Nerves.

UX notes

  • Instructions appear immediately in the left panel.
  • Immediate per-action feedback via shard glow, score bumps, and instability loss.
  • Buttons allow safe recovery: clear chain, chaos shuffle, full restart.

Originality angle

Rather than a static word guesser, this is a kinetic word-forging ritual. The challenge is not dictionary deduction alone; it is pressure management, ring reading, and execution under motion.
