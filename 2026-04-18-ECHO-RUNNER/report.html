ECHO RUNNER

Temporal Evasion Thriller | April 18, 2026 | Prototype Batch

🎯 Executive Summary

ECHO RUNNER is a high-speed reflex game with a unique twist: every move you make creates an "echo" that follows 3 seconds behind. Players must navigate a neon tunnel while avoiding collisions with their own past trajectory.

Core Innovation: The echo mechanic creates a "self-PvP" experience where players compete against their own previous actions. This creates emergent gameplay where early cautious play becomes dangerous later.

🎮 Core Game Loop

  1. Accelerate: Build speed while navigating obstacles
  2. Create Trail: Every position is recorded for echo generation
  3. Monitor: Watch the echo timer and minimap
  4. Evasion: Dodge echoes following your exact path
  5. Combo: Successfully dodge echoes to build multiplier
  6. Survive: Reach 5000m to escape

⚠️ Risk Assessment

MEDIUM: Difficulty curve - May be too punishing early. Need better first-time user experience.
MEDIUM: Echo visibility - Multiple echoes could become visually cluttered
HIGH: Novelty decay - Core mechanic may feel repetitive without variety (power-ups, different echo types)
LOW: Performance - Simple canvas rendering, scales well

🚀 Next Steps

  4. Add global leaderboards for distance and max combo
  5. Design boss encounters where echoes become predictable patterns
