Anagram Anvil — Strategy Report

Date: 2026-04-22 · Folder: 2026-04-22-anagram-anvil

Trend rationale: based on Wordle speedrun content plus latent anagram/word-game demand. The prototype turns wordplay into a pressure-cooker score attack.

Core loop

Tap letters off moving conveyors, assemble a word in the forge rack, submit, and repeat before overflow punishes the player.

  • Live badge unlocks
  • Best-score persistence
  • Clear win state at 360 and game-over state at 6 slag
