Letter Locomotive — Strategy Report

Trend anchor: letter-game. This prototype turns a familiar word-build mechanic into a themed timed-delivery loop with streak urgency and stronger feedback than a static crossword clone.

Core loop

Read the target/hint instantly → click letters into the wagon → submit fast for streak value → clear 4 rounds → cross 900 score to win.

Rewards and retention

Streak multiplierRound bonusBadge namingFast restarts

The deliberate pressure is not about impossible dictionaries; it is about readable goals and short cycle dopamine.

SEO / ASO / SEM

SEO: “timed word game”, “letter puzzle online”, “word builder browser game”.
ASO: show one screenshot of the rail bank and one of a successful delivery popup.
SEM: use motion creatives that show loading letters into a wagon before time expires.

KPIs

  • Round-1 solve time
  • % of runs with at least one correct streak chain
  • Win-rate after 5 sessions
  • Bank refresh usage rate

Experiments

  • Add daily themed dictionaries: cinema, football, memes, travel.
  • Introduce wildcard tiles earned by perfect rounds.
  • Test partial-credit scoring for near-miss anagrams.

Risks

  • Word pool is intentionally curated and small for prototype scope.
  • Without sound, the carriage fantasy is mostly visual/textual.
  • Advanced word players may want a larger dictionary mode quickly.
