🔊 RESONANCE RIFT

Game Design Report

🎯 Concept Overview

RESONANCE RIFT is a sound-wave manipulation puzzle where players emit pulses that bounce off dimensional walls. The core twist is the phase-shifting mechanic - players can switch between Alpha (pink/red) and Beta (cyan/blue) phases, revealing different walls and targets in each dimension.

Unique Selling Point: Dual-dimension puzzle solving where the same space contains different obstacles depending on your phase, requiring players to plan shots that may need to pass through "ghost" walls in one phase to hit targets in another.

Core Mechanics

  • Pulse Emission: Click/Space to fire a sound pulse from the emitter
  • Phase Shifting: Shift/Right-click to toggle between Alpha and Beta dimensions
  • Wall Bouncing: Pulses reflect off walls with realistic physics
  • Resonator Activation: Hit all targets to open the rift and advance
  • Limited Pulses: Strategic resource management - each level has limited shots

🎮 Gameplay Loop

  1. ANALYZE - Observe wall layout and resonator positions in current phase
  2. SHIFT - Toggle phases to see hidden geometry and plan trajectory
  3. AIM - Position mouse to direct pulse angle
  4. FIRE - Emit pulse and watch it bounce through the environment
  5. ADAPT - Use remaining pulses to hit missed targets
  6. ADVANCE - Clear all resonators to unlock the next rift

Progression System

  • 3 Hand-Crafted Tutorial Levels introducing mechanics gradually
  • Procedural Generation for infinite replayability beyond level 3
  • Increasing Complexity: More walls, more resonators, trickier layouts

🏆 Reward Systems

Scoring

  • Base hit: 100 points
  • Combo multiplier: x2, x3, x4... for consecutive hits with one pulse
  • Remaining pulse bonus: 50 points per unused pulse on level clear

Badge System

  • 🔊 Bounce Master: Achieve 5+ bounces on a single pulse
  • 🎯 Multi-Hit: Hit 3+ resonators with one pulse
  • ⭐ Precision: Complete level on first attempt without retrying

Visual Feedback

  • Trail effects showing pulse path history
  • Screen shake and glow on resonator activation
  • Combo popup animation for multi-hits
  • Phase shift visual transition
