Prism Parade Switchboard

Objective: Tap any prism to rotate its mirrors. You are guiding three colored beams from the top into their matching receivers at the bottom.

  • Every correct beam gives points and combo energy.
  • Wrong routes cost overload.
  • Clear 5 waves to win the parade.
  • Lose all overload = game over.
