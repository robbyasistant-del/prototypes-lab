🎮 Game Concept & Hook

One-Sentence Hook: "Race against your own ghosts - every run becomes part of the level in this 5-phase temporal platformer."

Core Concept: Echo Runner flips traditional platformer design on its head. Instead of memorizing a static level, players CREATE the level through their own actions. Each 10-second phase adds another "echo" of your previous run that becomes both platform and hazard.

Why It's Bold: This is a fundamentally different type of platformer. Most games punish mistakes; Echo Runner weaponizes your past successes. The level designer is YOU, 10 seconds ago. This creates unique "player vs self" psychology.

Unique Selling Points:

  • Procedural level generation based on player input (not algorithms)
  • Unique "player as level designer" mechanic
  • High-skill ceiling with emergent complexity
  • Spectator-friendly ghost racing creates content opportunities
  • Speedrun potential with infinite routing possibilities

🔄 Core Loop Explanation

The 5-Phase Structure

PHASE 1 → PHASE 2 (+1 ghost) → PHASE 3 (+2 ghosts) → PHASE 4 (+3 ghosts) → PHASE 5 (+4 ghosts)

1. Record (Phase 1): Player has clean platform layout. Must reach exit in 10 seconds. This recording becomes Ghost 1.

2. Echo 1 (Phase 2): Ghost 1 replays as solid platform/hazard. Player must use their own path while avoiding collisions.

3. Echo 2-4 (Phases 3-5): Each phase adds another ghost. By Phase 5, screen is chaos of 4 overlapping player paths.

4. Death & Retry: Hitting any ghost = instant death. R key restarts current phase. Deaths accumulate toward rank.

5. Victory: Complete all 5 phases. Rank S/A/B/C based on total deaths (0/3/6/10+).

Session Length: 2-5 minutes per level attempt | Skill Ceiling: Essentially infinite

⚠️ Risk Factors

🔴 Skill Wall

High difficulty may alienate 80%+ of players immediately. Most won't reach Phase 3.

✅ Mitigation

Add "Assist Mode" with slower ghosts or longer timers. Keep S rank for purists only.

🔴 Impossible RNG

A poorly-placed Phase 1 recording could make Phase 5 literally impossible.

✅ Mitigation

Level design must ensure ALL Phase 1 paths are completable with 4 ghosts. Extensive testing required.

🔴 Content Starvation

3 levels is 15-30 minutes for skilled players. Hardcores may complete and churn.

✅ Mitigation

Community level sharing would extend indefinitely. Leaderboards create replay value.

🔴 Frustration Quitting

Death on Phase 5 = restart Phase 5. Some ragequit on first failure.

✅ Mitigation

Consider checkpoint every 2 phases for accessibility mode. Optional for purists.
