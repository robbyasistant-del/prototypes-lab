Velvet Comet Heist — Product Report

A drag-and-aim action puzzle with a theatrical sci-fi skin. Players slingshot a velvet thief through vault rooms, collecting relics and bouncing off mirrors while avoiding security beams.

Strategy & Hook

The hook is tactile mastery. Drag, release, ricochet, snatch. The first move feels satisfying, but the real obsession comes from optimizing clean runs and threading impossible-looking vault angles.

Trend Rationale

  • Inspired by instant-play mobile behaviors: low-friction controls, short stages, perfect-line bragging rights.
  • Fits current appetite for stylish, GIF-able arcade moments.
  • Distinct from endless runners and word games in today's batch.

Core Loop

  1. Drag opposite the direction you want to launch.
  2. Bounce through the room, gather relics, dodge red beams.
  3. Reach the exit before energy runs out.
  4. Clear 4 rooms to win the run.

Rewards & Progression

  • Score from relic pickups, spare energy, and fast exits.
  • Badges: Clean Break, Triple Relic, Master Thief.
  • Win condition: clear 4 rooms.
  • Lose condition: energy hits zero or you hit too many lasers.

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • Keywords: ricochet heist game, slingshot puzzle action, browser arcade stealth.
  • Ad angle: “Can you rob the vault in a single velvet bounce?”
  • Store angle: stylish slingshot puzzle with dramatic escapes.

KPIs & Experiments

  • Average launch count per room.
  • Relic completion rate.
  • Restart rate after room-3 failures.
  • Future tests: daily vault seeds, gadget powers, mirror types.

Risks

  • Could benefit from more room variety over long sessions.
  • Touch precision must stay comfortable on small screens.
