Vowel Vault - Product Report

Strategy

Targets the strength of daily word games while fixing their passive pace. The player only controls vowels, which keeps entry friction low, but the rising flood and three simultaneous words create urgency and a real-time mastery curve.

SEO / ASO / SEM

  • SEO: word puzzle browser game, vowel game online, daily word rescue
  • ASO: “Save broken words before the vault floods.”
  • SEM angle: split-screen ads comparing slow crossword play vs. high-pressure vowel rescue.

Core Loop

  • Read three partial words
  • Select and place vowels into glowing gaps
  • Avoid wrong fits that raise flood
  • Clear rounds faster for score multipliers and badges
  • Chase flawless five-round wins

Rewards

  • Immediate +120 feedback on correct insertions
  • Round-clear bonus tied to speed and correctness
  • Three badges: Zero Mistakes, Fast Fingers, Vault Savior
  • Persistent best score for replay motivation

KPIs

  • Round 1 completion rate
  • Average flood level on death
  • Wrong vowel rate per session
  • Replay rate after narrow losses
  • Badge unlock distribution

Experiments

  • Daily seeded word packs for social comparison
  • Hard mode with consonant decoys
  • Language packs for ES/EN switching

Risks

  • If words are too obscure, speed tension feels unfair
  • Needs careful mobile touch hitboxes in later iterations
