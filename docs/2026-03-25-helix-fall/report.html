What was corrected

This pass makes the platform rule deterministic: cyan slices are solid and bounce the ball back up, only the black gap allows a pass-through and clear, and red still kills on contact.

What changed in r11 solid-sweep

  • Swept collision resolution replaced the old single late frame check.
  • Substep downward movement means crossed rows are processed in order instead of being skipped.
  • Exact crossing-time angle sampling decides whether the ball hit cyan, black gap, or red.
  • Safe contact snap + bounce corrects position to the platform top and immediately reverses vertical velocity.
  • Gap-only clears preserve scoring and break effects only when the opening is actually used.
  • Visible version bump to v2026.03.26-r11 solid-sweep.

Why this is more reliable

The old logic could still answer collision too late in the frame, which left room for tunneling through cyan. The new sweep resolves every crossed platform before the ball can move past it, so solid slices behave like solid floors.

Verification target

Local verification checks should confirm: cyan bounce works without tunneling, the black gap clears and scores, red kills, and both docs mirrors match the working prototype.
